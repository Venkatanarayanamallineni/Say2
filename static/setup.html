Under the hood.

No vector database, no training. A small business site fits in NVIDIA Nemotron's long memory, so every answer is written with the whole site in view.

  1. Crawl

    Up to 50 public pages, plus booking and ordering links.

  2. Read PDFs

    Menus, price lists and schedules, line by line.

  3. Rank

    Pages with prices first, up to about 250,000 characters.

  4. NVIDIA Nemotron

    Nemotron 3 Super on Nebius Token Factory reads it all and writes the answer.

  5. Sourced reply

    Owner answers first, a link to the source, and gaps go to the inbox.
