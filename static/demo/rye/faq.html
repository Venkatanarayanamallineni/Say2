Good to know

Allergies

Everything is baked in one kitchen that uses wheat, rye, eggs, dairy, sesame and tree nuts. We can't promise any item is free of allergens.

Gluten-free

We don't make gluten-free bread or pastries. Our flour is in the air all day.

Vegan

Our country sourdough, seeded rye and rosemary focaccia are vegan. The pastries are not.

Day-old bread

Yesterday's loaves are half price, on the rack by the door, until they're gone.

Paying

We take cash and all major cards. No checks.

Parking

Free parking in the small lot behind the bakery, entrance on Mill Lane.

Seating

We're a takeout bakery with one bench outside. No indoor seating.

Jobs

We hire early-morning bakers each fall. Email jobs@riseandrye.example.
