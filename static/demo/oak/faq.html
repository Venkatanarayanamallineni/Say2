Questions

Is there parking?

Yes, free parking in the lot behind the building.

Do you have showers and lockers?

Yes. Bring your own lock, or borrow one at the front desk.

Can I bring a friend?

Members can bring one guest free twice a month.

How old do I have to be?

16 and up. Ages 14 and 15 can work out with a parent or guardian.

Can I bring my dog?

Only service animals are allowed inside.
